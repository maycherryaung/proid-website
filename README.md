This is a collection of small websites I made for my Sem 3.1 module, "Project ID". Each of these websites represent a stage in an escape challenge, and are connected to the next one.
The aim of the escape challenge is to inform youths about online harms and encourage them to carry out IMDA's four online safety actions: Set, Think, Report, and Engage & Support.
In index.html, players will have to profile Rachel from her public Instagram account to get the key for the next round.
In zone2.html, they will find forensic proof of a post spreading false information.
In zone3.html, they will take action to report this post and learn about Online Safety Commission (OSC) Singapore.
In zone4.html, they will co-create a safe-zone for the victim of online harm.
